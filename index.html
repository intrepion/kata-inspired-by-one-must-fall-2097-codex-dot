FIELD NOTES / RULES & RESEARCH +

Standing guard stops standing and aerial strikes. Low guard stops standing and low strikes. Throws break guard; jumping avoids throws and sweeps. Stability drains on impact and returns during neutral movement; depletion causes a mash-recoverable stun. Red integrity never regenerates. Sixty-second rounds, first to two. No chip damage.
