# Rivet Circuit
A robot exhibition fighting sport.

## Language
**Integrity**: Remaining damage capacity; zero loses a round. _Avoid_: energy.
**Stability**: Resistance to becoming stunned, restored during neutral movement. _Avoid_: attack mana, stamina cost.
**Block**: Facing-relative defense against an incoming attack at an appropriate height.
**Throw**: A close standing grapple that defeats guarding.
**Round**: One bout ending at knockout or time limit.
**Match**: A first-to-two-rounds contest.
**Arc drive**: A forward-lunging special initiated by a quarter-circle motion and punch.
